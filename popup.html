Help

CookieBuddy looks at the current page and checks whether cookies or third-party requests still happen after you choose "deny all".

  • It tries to recognize the cookie banner provider.
  • It lists visible cookies and likely services.
  • It compares what happens before and after opt-out.
  • If something remains, treat it as a review signal, not as a final legal finding.

Privacy

CookieBuddy does not track you and does not send scan results to the creator or to any analytics service.

  • Scans run locally in your browser.
  • The last scan is stored only in Chrome local extension storage on your device.
  • There is no analytics, tracking pixel, remote logging, or account system.
  • External links and drafted emails only open when you click them; those external services have their own privacy rules.

Open source

CookieBuddy is intended to be open source. Contributions, bug reports, and banner-detection improvements are welcome.
